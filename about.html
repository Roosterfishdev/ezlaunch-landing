About EZLaunch

EZLaunch is a static hosting platform for websites generated with AI tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, Bolt, Lovable, Replit, and v0. It is built and operated by Jaguar Hosting LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, USA.

Why EZLaunch exists

AI coding tools make it easy to generate a complete website in minutes, but getting that site online still means picking a host, configuring builds, wiring up SSL, and finding separate services for forms and analytics. EZLaunch removes those steps. You drop a ZIP file or connect a GitHub repository, and the site is live with HTTPS, a custom domain, form handling, and analytics included.

Who builds it

EZLaunch is built by a small team led by founder Julian Cabada, who also writes the EZLaunch blog. The EZLaunch CLI is open source on GitHub.
